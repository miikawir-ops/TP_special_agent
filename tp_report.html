The ITAT Mumbai ruled that Slack SaaS subscription fees are not considered royalties under Section 9(1)(vi) of Indian tax law or the India-Ireland DTAA. The tribunal held these fees constitute business receipts, provided there is no permanent establishment in India for the recipient.
