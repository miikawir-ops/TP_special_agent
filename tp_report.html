TP Signal of the Day

IRS struggles to apply economic substance in transfer pricing cases.

The IRS's difficulty in successfully leveraging the economic substance doctrine for transfer pricing adjustments suggests limitations in applying broader tax principles to specific intercompany transactions. This highlights the unique challenges of aligning the arm's length principle with the more subjective "economic substance" test. Companies facing IRS audits should understand the specific thresholds and evidentiary burdens required to establish economic substance, preparing robust documentation that substantiates both the arm's length nature and the commercial rationale of their intercompany dealings.
