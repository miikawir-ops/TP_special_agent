TP Signal of the Day

Indian ITAT: Exclude COVID-19 extraordinary costs from TP operating expenses.

This Indian ITAT ruling provides a strong precedent, asserting that extraordinary, non-recurring costs, such as those from the COVID-19 pandemic, should be excluded from operating expenses for transfer pricing comparability analyses. It reinforces the arm's length principle by ensuring comparisons are not distorted by unique, exceptional events. Practically, multinationals should re-evaluate their pandemic-era intercompany transactions and profitability, leveraging this guidance to support comparability adjustments for periods affected by similar economic shocks or unique events.
